GoPay - Help & Developer Info

We’ve all been in that situation where getting transport becomes a real struggle—not because there are no taxis, but because of one thing: cash. You find yourself standing, waiting… hoping for the right taxi to come along. Which is something that is not just time-consuming, but sometimes, can even cause you to raise your transport fare to an amount which will be more acceptable to give change. Do you know how it feels to pay 250 FCFA for a ride that should normally cost 200 FCFA? It hurts. Just because you need change.

There are days when you completely forget your allowance or wallet at home. Even though you have money in your mobile account, the driver has neither Orange Money nor MTN Mobile Money. That’s exactly what we want to change.

My name is Tiagho Momo, and I am here with my Scrum Master Ms. Okala Francesca. Today, we present to you: GoPay — a mobile platform built to simplify and secure transport payments.

1. Simplicity & User-Friendliness

GoPay is intuitive for both drivers and clients. When a driver signs up, a QR code is automatically generated. The driver prints and places this code in the car. Clients scan and pay instantly — no complicated interaction. The driver simply receives a notification.

2. Flexibility & Fair Pricing

What if you’re on Orange and the driver accepts only MTN? GoPay solves this with smart switching. We hold accounts with both MTN and Orange. You pay through your network, and GoPay ensures the driver receives funds in theirs. No confusion. No delay.

Plus, no more extra charges! GoPay uses a merchant payment model — like restaurant QR codes — to make your payment fee-free. You pay exactly the fare. No more, no less.

3. Speed

GoPay is fast. Scan, pay, and go — all within seconds. That’s the experience we offer.

4. Security

We never ask for your PIN. All transactions are done via secure USSD prompts. You approve payments directly through your provider.

5. Development & Scrum

Since our arrival at the ICT University, we’ve worked on several projects using the Waterfall methodology. But thanks to your guidance, we’ve embraced Agile and Scrum. This shift has been transformative, allowing us to build a more robust, user-friendly, and feature-complete application in less time.
